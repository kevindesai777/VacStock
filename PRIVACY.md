# VacStock privacy policy

Effective October 7, 2026

VacStock is an Android app, made by Kevin Desai, for keeping count of the vaccine doses a clinic
has in stock. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What the app stores

The app stores the list you build: vaccine names and how many doses of each are in stock. The list
is saved in the app's private storage on your device.

VacStock is meant for stock counts only. Please don't type patient names or other personal details
into it.

## What the app doesn't do

- It doesn't collect, send or share any data. The app doesn't have permission to use the internet.
- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- It doesn't ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, files or anything else on your
  device.

## Android backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android includes the app's list in your device backup to
your Google Account, the same as it does for other apps, so the list can come back when you set up
a new phone. Google stores and manages that backup under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer can't see it. You can
turn backup off in your device settings.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any vaccine from the list at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its
storage in your device's app settings, removes everything the app stored on your device. Backed-up
copies are managed in your Google Account.

## Children

VacStock is a tool for clinics and isn't directed at children.
